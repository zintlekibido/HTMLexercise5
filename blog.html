Review: Clearblue Digital Pregnancy Test Technology

The Clearblue Digital Pregnancy Test uses built-in smart technology to replace inadequate paper lines with a clear screen. Inside the plastic holder is a high-tech paper strip containing specific particles that bind to human chorionic gonadotropin (hCG); the unique hormone your body produces during pregnancy. An optical sensor measures exact hormone levels by passing light through the test strip. An internal chip automatically analyzes the light pattern, eliminating human error to display a clear "Pregnant" or "Not Pregnant" result.

Review: Clearblue Ovulation Test

The Clearblue Advanced Digital Ovulation Test is a smart tracking device that maps your unique monthly fertility window. Unlike basic paper strips, its digital reader monitors two key fertility hormones simultaneously to identify your peak days. Results are shown as easy-to-read digital smiley faces, making biological data tracking incredibly simple and actionable.
